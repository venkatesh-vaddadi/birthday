Radha-Krishna Vrindavan Birthday Website

Music: local Radha-Krishna Naamawali, strictly limited to 8:16 (496 seconds).
The music progress slider is draggable and seeks freely within the 8:16 track.
Vrindavan Memories uses three square-cropped images supplied by the user.
Rose/flower petal shower is restored to a visible, colorful level.

Mac:
1. Open Terminal.
2. cd into this folder.
3. Run: python3 -m http.server 8000
4. Open: http://localhost:8000
